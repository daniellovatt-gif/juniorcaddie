Left-handed note
Left-handed junior clubs are made in far smaller numbers than right-handed, so choice is genuinely more limited. US Kids Golf and PING both produce left-handed junior options, but check current stock before assuming a specific size or set is available. Second-hand left-handed sets are rarer too — worth buying a good one when you find it on the marketplace rather than waiting for a perfect match.
